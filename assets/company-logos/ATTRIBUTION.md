# Company logos

Original brand marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) `16.33.0`. Each file is the Simple Icons path recoloured with that brand's own hex, so it is not a byte-for-byte copy of the upstream file. Generated by `scripts/logos/vendor.mjs`; do not edit by hand.

Every brand, its hex, its upstream source URL and its licence are recorded in `manifest.json`.

## Not CC0

- **Broadcom** (`broadcom`) — custom. Source: https://www.broadcom.com/support
- **Canonical** (`canonical`) — CC-BY-SA-3.0. Source: https://design.ubuntu.com/resources

## Coverage

205 of the companies in the catalogue have a mark here. The rest keep a generated monogram: Simple Icons has no entry for them, and no free bulk source carries original artwork for the long tail. See `scripts/logos/README.md`.
