# Stitch interface

The current theme follows the second Stitch export’s Nexus Enterprise UI: horizontal navigation, a PrepYard wordmark with a serif accent, charcoal surfaces, white primary actions, mint/blue progress colors, subtle ambient glows, and rounded panels.

The dashboard reads real student progress. The ring shows solved divided by attempted plus solved, rather than a simulated daily goal. No sample streaks, premium memberships, live sync statuses, or retention percentages are shown. Recorded weekly revision activity uses India time.

Company search, sorting, pagination, stored logos, frequency windows, completion, revision confidence/history, and private notes remain connected to the existing services. Company cards can expand inline or open their dedicated sheet. Practice retains Topics, Patterns, and Collections browsing modes and separate question tool columns.

Desktop navigation sits in a sticky top bar. On smaller screens it becomes a keyboard-accessible menu with Escape dismissal and restored focus. Ctrl/Cmd+K focuses quick search. Revision becomes a mobile bottom sheet; notes remain collapsed until expanded and support deletion.
